Tips for a closer clone
  • Clean speech beats long speech: 8–20 seconds with no music, echo or other voices.
  • The clip’s mood carries over — for a calm read, use a calm clip.
  • Same language in the clip and the text gives the strongest accent match.
  • Not quite right? Make 3 takes and keep the best, or nudge expressiveness below.
  • Only clone voices you have permission to use.
