Connect your AI assistant

Use Story Studio from Claude or ChatGPT — make pictures, speech and songs, turn them into videos, and regenerate scenes just by asking. You sign in with your Story Studio username and password; there is no separate key.

1 Copy the server address

{{ base }}/mcp

2 Add it to your app

Claude (claude.ai or desktop)

  1. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector
  2. Name Story Studio, paste the address
  3. Press Connect and sign in

ChatGPT

  1. Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced: turn on Developer mode
  2. Create a connector: name Story Studio, paste the address, authentication OAuth
  3. Sign in, then switch it on in a chat from the + menu

Claude Code

claude mcp add --transport http --scope user story-studio {{ base }}/mcp

Then type /mcp, pick story-studio and choose Authenticate.

Shortcut: tell your assistant Install {{ base }}/install and it will set itself up, or tell you the clicks it can't do for you.

3 Just ask

  • “Make a picture of a lighthouse at sunset, landscape.”
  • “Same picture, but in a storm at night.”
  • “Turn these three pictures into a short video about a stormy night.”
  • “Regenerate scene 2 of my last video with a slower camera.”
  • “Read this paragraph aloud in a warm, slow voice.”
  • “Write and sing a Hindi lullaby about the moon, soft piano.”

Pictures and speech take about 10–30 seconds; videos about 2 minutes per 5 seconds. Everything counts against your account's hourly limits, and an assistant's access ends when your account does.
