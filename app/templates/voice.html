    2 Train

    Training shares the GPU with everyone else, so it runs in short slices — usually 20–40 minutes in all, longer on a busy day. You can leave this page.

    Your voice profile

    Measured from your singing. It is how new songs are pitched into your range and steered toward your manner (tone, vibrato). The voice model and this profile are kept until you delete the voice.

    Your singing style (optional, advanced)

    Your voice model makes songs sound like you. A singing style also teaches the song model how you sing — your phrasing and delivery. It needs at least 3 full songs (8+ minutes) of you singing, 10–20 is better, each with its lyrics. Training needs most of the GPU for up to about 90 minutes, so it starts as soon as the card is free.

    Lyrics are drafted automatically — check each one and press Confirm. Wrong lyrics teach wrong pronunciation.

      3 Use your voice

      The song's lead vocal is re-sung in your voice and mixed back onto the original music. It keeps the original singer's melody and phrasing — your voice, their performance.

      The song is written and performed in your range and manner, then sung in your exact voice.

      Or write the lyrics yourself

      If you uploaded speaking recordings, they set the accent and pace; otherwise it starts from a preset speaker in your range.
